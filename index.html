Why collapse channels

  1. Cut complexityThree channels become one.
  2. Shrink storageThe raw array drops by two-thirds.
  3. Speed up pipelinesLess data for every downstream pass.
  4. Foreground structureEdges and texture stand out once color stops competing for attention.
